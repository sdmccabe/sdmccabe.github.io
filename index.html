Stefan D. McCabe

I am a Senior Data Scientist at Alethea. From 2022 to 2024, I was a postdoctoral associate at George Washington University’s Institute for Data, Democracy & Politics, working with Matthew Hindman and Andrew Ifedapo Thompson. My research focuses on developing methods for accurately measuring and describing online social behaviors. I received my Ph.D. from Northeastern University’s Network Science Institute in 2022, where I was advised by David Lazer. I received my master's degree from George Mason University’s Department of Computational Social Science, where I was advised by Rob Axtell. In summer 2020, I was a research intern at Microsoft Research, working with David Rothschild.
